# PROJECT_RULES.md

## Authority, Version Boundary, and Project Identity

This document is the binding scientific and operational contract for PF-ERI from 2026-07-10 onward. It replaces every earlier rule, freeze note, dashboard, manuscript sentence, protocol, phase label, result table, or generated claim when those materials conflict with this document. Older material remains preserved as provenance and exploratory evidence; it is not deleted, relabelled as confirmatory, or used to satisfy a PF-ERI v2 requirement.

PF-ERI v2 studies a narrow but consequential problem in wildlife individual re-identification. A strong descriptor can retrieve two visually similar images, yet that similarity may not constitute adequate evidence for a responsible human comparison. PF-ERI is therefore a post-retrieval, pair-level evidence-admission layer. It estimates whether a candidate pair contains sufficiently comparable identity evidence to be admitted for expert review, sent to review with caution, deferred, or marked non-comparable. The binding scientific line is: **Similarity is not admissibility.**

PF-ERI is not a new descriptor, a replacement for a Re-ID system, an automatic animal-identity assignment system, an image-quality filter, or a universal cross-species threshold. Its potential contribution is the measurement and decision protocol that connects pairwise visual evidence, calibrated uncertainty, asymmetric review costs, and an auditable review action.

The v1 evidence package is exploratory only. The current 400 reviewed rows, their derived model comparisons, routing outputs, reviewer-agreement summaries, and manuscript figures based on them must be labelled `v1_exploratory_not_confirmatory`. They may be used for error analysis, feature design, sample simulation, and historical narrative, but never as a v2 confirmation set, a calibration set, a reviewer-reliability proof, or a deployable performance claim. The synthetic reliability result is prohibited from human-rater reporting. The claimed external-review result remains `provenance_pending` until raw source records, reviewer attestation, interface version, timestamps, and a reproducible linkage to the reviewed pairs have been independently verified.

## The Research Question and Its Claim Boundary

The primary v2 question is whether automatically available pair-evidence features add practically useful information about blinded human reviewability after descriptor similarity and independently measured image-quality controls have already been considered. The primary empirical domain is known-identity CzechLynx candidate pairs. Human reviewability, also called evidential admissibility, is the endpoint. It asks whether the two images contain sufficient comparable visual evidence for a responsible individual-level comparison; it does not ask the reviewer to assign the animal's identity. A different-identity pair can be review-ready when its visible evidence permits a confident rejection.

The primary public claim is conditional on confirmation: in a pre-specified CzechLynx candidate queue, automatically available pair-evidence features can improve the assessment and routing of human-reviewable evidence beyond descriptor similarity and independent image-quality measurements. The claim is local to the data contract, descriptors, review protocol, and decision costs that are actually evaluated. It is not a claim of identity accuracy, mAP, MRR, top-k superiority, population inference, field deployment, or distribution-free safety.

The methodological position is grounded in the project’s existing literature record, particularly `sources/2026-06-17_pferi_gap_model_map.md` and `sources/2026-06-17_pferi_research_gap_workflow_assessment.md`. Those records establish that animal Re-ID descriptors, biometric quality, occlusion-aware matching, selective prediction, and human-in-the-loop wildlife workflows already exist. V2 must therefore describe its novelty as the auditable instantiation of pair-level evidence admission for patterned-felid candidate review, not as invention of any of those broader ideas.

## V2 Study Architecture

V2 begins with a frozen strong-descriptor candidate queue and converts every candidate comparison into one canonical unordered pair. The canonical-pair record must retain both query-direction memberships, descriptor membership, rank, similarity, source image identifiers, known identity where permitted, and inclusion probability. A physical image pair may be reviewed only once even if it occurs in several descriptor queues. Duplicate directions, repeat rows, and conflicting historical labels must be recorded rather than silently collapsed. No model, bootstrap, or final count may treat duplicate directions as independent observations.

The study has two distinct confirmatory samples. The mechanism-confirmation sample will contain 400 unique unordered CzechLynx pairs, with pre-specified coverage of descriptor family, similarity/rank, independently measured image quality, laterality or viewpoint, visible evidence state, known same/different identity status, camera/site where available, and day/night or infrared state where available. Its purpose is to test whether the proposed measurements vary, remain reliable, and capture the proposed evidence mechanisms. It is deliberately not a deployment-prevalence sample.

The deployment-queue confirmation sample will contain 800 additional unique unordered CzechLynx pairs, probability-sampled from the frozen descriptor candidate queue with inclusion probabilities retained in the audit manifest. It will include 400 memberships from each primary descriptor, subject to deduplication across descriptors. Its purpose is to estimate calibrated probabilities, risk–coverage behaviour, review budgets, and human-time consequences under a realistic candidate queue. Mechanism and deployment samples may share the same broad source pool but may not share a pair. Their results may not be unweighted pooled for a deployment claim. The formal target is therefore 1,200 unique unordered pairs, not 1,200 rows or 1,200 descriptor directions.

The target may be reduced only before any v2 outcome review begins, and only through a dated power-and-cost simulation that records the anticipated cluster structure, missingness, minimum practical increment, reviewer availability, and the new target. A reduction cannot be justified by an observed outcome, a desired effect direction, or exhaustion after reviewing a selective subset. The target may be increased through the same process. A complete v2 study is not considered launched until the exact sample-size decision and sampling seed are committed to the protocol repository.

## Separation of Measurement, Outcome Review, and Identity Truth

Feature measurement and outcome review are distinct jobs. The feature team may record observable facts such as detector bounding-box coverage, image dimensions, sharpness or exposure estimates, estimated visible body regions, laterality or viewpoint, occlusion, infrared state, visible patterned-area estimates, local-match coverage, descriptor disagreement, feature missingness, and automated inference failures. The feature team must not make a review-ready decision, see an outcome label, or adapt a feature definition after seeing v2 outcome results.

The outcome-review team sees only the pair images and the review instrument. It must not see descriptor identity, descriptor score, rank, PF-ERI score, feature values, feature annotations, route, sampling stratum, known same/different identity truth, earlier labels, or prior comments. Every v2 pair receives two independent blinded first-pass reviews. A third blinded adjudicator reviews only pre-specified disagreement cases, without seeing the first-pass labels. Reviewer identifiers, interface version and hash, session timestamps, raw form entries, image display transformation, and assignment records are immutable source artifacts. A majority or adjudicated label is derived data and can never replace the raw review logs.

Known identity truth is held in a separate access-controlled analysis file. It may be used for sample stratification before review, for planned same/different subgroup analyses after label lock, and for the consequential-validity experiment. It must remain absent from the reviewer interface and the reviewer-facing export. The review instrument must be tested with a deliberate leakage audit before collection. A single visible score, filename, rank, or hidden sortable column that exposes the assignment condition invalidates the affected review batch until a documented remediation decision is made.

## Measurement Feasibility Gate

Before the 1,200-pair confirmation collection, the project must run a separately labelled measurement-feasibility study on a new, difficult-case pilot. The pilot measures feature variation, missingness, inter-rater reliability for human-measured features, agreement between automatic and human measurements, and the time and failure rate of every inference component. It is not a model-development shortcut and cannot be merged into the confirmation samples.

The measurement protocol must state, before its pilot outcomes are reviewed, which features are core, which are optional, how an unavailable observation is represented, and what reliability or variation condition causes a feature to be removed or redefined. A constant feature, a feature with unreliable measurement, or a feature available only by asking the outcome reviewer for a subjective judgement is not an admissible PF-ERI v2 predictor. It may remain as a qualitative error-analysis note. Manually measured structural features are measurement truth and an oracle upper bound; they cannot support a workflow-efficiency claim unless an equivalent automatic acquisition path and its cost are reported.

## Data Isolation, Development, Calibration, and Confirmation

V2 has three non-overlapping information roles. The development partition is used for feature engineering, model-family selection, error analysis, and the pre-specified comparison of a small number of model forms. The calibration partition is used only to fit probability calibration, choose the route threshold, and select the cost-sensitive policy under the previously stated loss. The confirmation partition is revealed once and is used for the final estimate. Any feature, threshold, model, cost policy, or sampling change informed by confirmation data begins PF-ERI v3 and requires a new unseen confirmation set.

Partitioning must be performed on the image-pair graph and identity graph, not by random individual rows. The same physical image and the same unordered pair may not cross development, calibration, and confirmation boundaries. Confirmation must additionally include an identity-disjoint sensitivity analysis whenever the available identity graph permits it. Every split, component count, identity allocation, seed, and exclusion is written to a versioned manifest before outcome labels are used for modelling. No data split is valid merely because a script completed without an error.

## Primary Model, Endpoint, and Applied-Mathematics Contract

The primary model is a regularized, interpretable probabilistic model for the probability that a candidate pair is review-ready. Its principal input families are descriptor similarity, independent automatically measured image quality, and automatically available pair-evidence features. The primary incremental contrast is the full model against the descriptor-plus-independent-quality active control on exactly the same confirmation rows. A pre-specified nonlinear model may be reported as a secondary robustness comparison, but it cannot replace the primary model after observing a favourable result.

The primary endpoint is a blinded binary reviewability label, defined as `review_ready` versus `not_ready_or_uncertain`. The primary confirmatory performance measure is the change in Brier score between the active control and the full model, accompanied by a cluster-aware 95% interval. Before packet generation, the pre-specified analysis plan must record the minimum practical Brier-score increment, the noninferiority or superiority rule, the power simulation, the single principal cost/budget region, and the multiplicity hierarchy. Those numerical values are not to be selected from v2 results. AUROC, AUPRC, log loss, calibration error, calibration plots, descriptor-specific effects, and alternate budgets are secondary or descriptive unless explicitly promoted before review begins.

The decision layer uses a three-action, asymmetric-loss policy: `admit`, `expert_review`, or `defer`. Its loss function must separately record the cost of an incorrect admission, unnecessary expert review, an unnecessary defer, automated-feature computation, human feature correction where used, and reviewer minutes. The project must not invent a conservation-dollar cost. It may report sensitivity regions across transparent cost ratios and directly measured human-time costs. A formal conformal or distribution-free risk claim is prohibited unless its assumptions, calibration split, loss, coverage definition, and finite-sample guarantee are all demonstrated on the relevant data contract.

## Consequential Validity and Workflow Evidence

In addition to the construct-label study, V2 includes an independent consequential-validity experiment on a pre-specified sample from the deployment queue. New blinded reviewers receive the same images and make a known-identity same/different judgement, with confidence and elapsed time recorded. They do not generate the primary reviewability labels and do not see PF-ERI routes. The analysis tests whether pairs that the locked policy admits, sends to review, or defers differ in judgement reliability, confidence, or time. This is the bridge from the latent construct to a meaningful human workflow outcome. It does not convert the project into an automatic identity classifier.

The workflow claim succeeds only if the locked policy improves the specified risk or time objective in the representative deployment sample relative to the descriptor-based active policy, after the full cost of automated feature extraction and human correction is counted. A lower not-ready rate inside a score-enriched case-control sample is not workflow evidence. If feature acquisition costs eliminate the benefit, the result must be reported as a measurement result rather than an efficiency tool.

## Success, Failure, and Stopping Rules

V2 may support its highest CzechLynx claim only when the measurement gate passes, blinded outcome labels are auditable, the full model meets the pre-registered primary incremental rule in the confirmation partition, calibration and route performance meet their locked criteria in the deployment sample, and the result remains directionally compatible under the planned graph-aware and identity-disjoint sensitivities. The report must show the size and uncertainty of the increment, not merely a pass/fail statement. A pooled positive effect must not hide a pre-specified harmful or null effect in an important descriptor, camera, laterality, day/night, missingness, or reviewer stratum.

V2 fails or is narrowed when a core feature is constant or unreliable, blinding fails, outcome agreement indicates that the construct is not stably measured, the full model fails the primary active-control comparison, the effect disappears in the representative queue or planned dependency sensitivity, calibration repeatedly exceeds the locked risk threshold, or costs outweigh the routing benefit. Failure is a scientific outcome. The appropriate response is to publish a measurement or negative-result account, archive the exact failure mechanism, and open PF-ERI v3 only with revised features and newly collected confirmation data. It is never acceptable to retune the v2 confirmation set until it becomes positive.

## External Species, Bobcat, and the General Tool Claim

Bobcat is not a v2 empirical validation domain. Existing Bobcat manifests, candidate queues, and deterministic routing outputs are provenance or engineering smoke tests only. They cannot be described as transfer validation, identity validation, reviewability validation, calibration, or a field-readiness result. A Bobcat study may start only after a licensed and auditable image source, a frozen pair manifest, independent blinded pair labels, and a species-specific adapter protocol exist. Identity-related claims additionally require verified individual identities or a separately audited same/different reference set.

Mainland Clouded Leopard is the preferred future conservation case because a compatible, auditable known-identity or pair-label source would permit the strongest extension of the CzechLynx logic. Marbled Cat remains a valuable second case because it tests a rarer conservation context, but rarity alone is not a substitute for lawful data, pair labels, or review access. Neither species may appear as an empirical v2 result before its data and review contract is complete.

The long-term tool may be broadly reusable only at the interface level. Its stable core is the canonical pair contract, route action space, loss accounting, calibration protocol, audit manifest, and explanation record. Each species requires its own adapter for detection, body layout, laterality, patterned evidence regions, camera modality, feature failures, local calibration, and validation. The project must never call this a universal model or universal threshold.

No text may use `blind-confirmed`, `validated`, `ready for deployment`, `risk guaranteed`, or `pass` for v2 before the corresponding source artifacts exist.

## Documentation, Code, Data, and Git Discipline

Documentation should update the authoritative documents above rather than create a new report for each operational task. Durable protocols, decision records, final scientific reports, and replacement navigation documents are appropriate additions. Scratch audits and generated inventories belong in temporary or output locations and should not become competing sources of scientific truth.

Use CodeGraph first when locating or understanding code in this indexed repository. CodeGraph can answer where code is and how it is connected; it cannot decide which photographs are valid, which rows exist, whether a reviewer was blind, whether a feature varied, or which scientific phase is correct. Those questions require direct inspection of manifests, raw forms, audits, counts, and these project rules. A broad retrieval result that returns a legacy CzechLynx or Bobcat script is a retrieval miss, not a scientific conclusion.

Scripts must be small, auditable, deterministic where appropriate, and explicit about input manifests, seeds, row counts, deduplication, exclusion reasons, and pass/fail conditions. New training code is not an active priority unless it serves the locked v2 comparison and preserves the development/calibration/confirmation boundary. Raw images, internal mappings, output tables, and review logs remain uncommitted unless the project owner explicitly authorizes otherwise. Documentation and scripts may be committed with clear messages after their content has been verified.
