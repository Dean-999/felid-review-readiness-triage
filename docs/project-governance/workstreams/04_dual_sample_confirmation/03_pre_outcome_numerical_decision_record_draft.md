# Pre-Outcome Numerical Decision Record — Working Draft

Status: primary rule, four-stage allocation, dependence/interval specification, reviewer-operation design, and sampling-strata design accepted pre-outcome; official seed and image-role allocation frozen, while formal pair sampling remains blocked.  
Applies to: Workstream 04 Task 03.

The project owner accepted the primary estimand and decision direction on 15 July 2026 before any v2 outcome packet was created. The estimand is the mean paired difference `Brier(active control) − Brier(full automatic-evidence model)` on identical confirmation rows, so positive values favor the full automatic-evidence model. The primary rule is superiority. The model can meet the primary rule only if the lower endpoint of the pre-specified graph-aware 95% interval is greater than 0.005. This is a minimum practical improvement, not an assumed true v2 effect, a result from v1, or a value selected from a favorable sensitivity row.

The project owner also confirmed that the three opaque codes used in the v2 timed operational rehearsal represented three different people. The archived return audit records 72 completed outcome-free tasks, 24 for each code, with all tasks matching the restricted allocation and with no technical interruption. This confirms the limited operational claim described in the Task 03 addendum. It does not supply a completion-rate forecast for formal judgements, a formal-review time estimate, an adjudication rate, a collection-window commitment, or any human-label reliability evidence.

The project owner accepted a four-stage analytical allocation on 15 July 2026 before any v2 outcome packet was created. The 3,000 candidate images will be assigned to three image-disjoint information roles: 1,000 development images, 1,000 calibration images, and 1,000 confirmation images. Development outcomes may support feature engineering, model-family comparison, regularisation selection, and error analysis. Calibration outcomes may support probability calibration and action-threshold fitting after the development model is frozen. Confirmation outcomes may not influence either process.

The accepted analyzable targets are 400 development pairs, 400 calibration pairs, 400 mechanism-confirmation pairs, and 800 deployment-confirmation pairs. A conservative 0.90 planning completion fraction gives separate planned collection counts of 445, 445, 445, and 889 after applying `ceil(target / 0.90)`. The programme therefore prepares 2,224 unique unordered pairs to protect 2,000 analyzable pairs. If every prepared pair receives two first-pass reviews, the pre-adjudication workload is 4,448 judgements. The 0.90 value is a planning choice rather than an estimate from the 72-task outcome-free interface rehearsal.

The image partitions must have zero physical-image and canonical-pair crossing. Mechanism-confirmation and deployment-confirmation pairs must also be mutually exclusive inside the confirmation image partition. Both confirmation samples may be released only after a common model-and-analysis freeze, so an attractive mechanism result cannot trigger model revision before deployment confirmation. Their distinct sampling roles remain unchanged: the mechanism sample supports pre-specified conditional mechanism and failure analyses; the representative deployment sample supports the primary paired Brier comparison, calibration, risk, coverage, time, and cost estimates.

The project owner accepted the conservative dependence and interval specification on 15 July 2026 before any v2 outcome packet was created. The binding planning calculation will use a paired Brier-loss-difference standard deviation of 0.120 and a joint image-endpoint variance fraction of 0.10. Under the declared planning model, two pair observations sharing one physical image therefore have an induced correlation of 0.05. The primary deployment point estimate will be the Hájek-normalized inverse-probability-weighted paired Brier difference when inclusion probabilities are unequal and the ordinary paired mean when the final deployment sample is self-weighting. Its primary 95% interval will use the dyadic cluster-robust sandwich variance estimator with physical image IDs as the shared dyad members. The 400-pair mechanism-confirmation sample will not be pooled into that primary deployment estimate.

This conservative specification exposes, rather than conceals, the precision limit of the accepted 800-pair deployment sample. The outcome-free planning approximation gives an effective pair count of about 755.9, a standard error of about 0.00436, and an approximately 0.0172 true Brier improvement requirement for an 80% chance of clearing the lower-bound threshold. These quantities are operating characteristics of the frozen assumptions, not forecasts of the model result and not grounds for weakening the success rule after outcomes are observed.

The project owner accepted the reviewer-operation design on 15 July 2026 before any v2 outcome packet was created. Every one of the 2,224 prepared pairs will receive two independent first-pass judgements from different people. Each disagreement will be adjudicated by a third person who did not first-pass that pair, and no analytical block may receive a reduced review standard. The design does not assign an expected judgement duration, expected adjudication rate, total-hour budget, or collection window. Actual elapsed time and the actual adjudication proportion will be reported from immutable operational logs after collection.

The project owner accepted the sampling-strata design on 16 July 2026 before any official seed, image assignment, pair sample, or v2 outcome packet existed. Images will be blocked by outcome-free graph degree and retained automatic quality state before exact 1,000-image development, calibration, and confirmation allocation. Development uses balanced retrieval-and-evidence coverage; calibration uses proportional retrieval-stratum sampling; deployment confirmation uses proportional probability sampling from the frozen canonical confirmation frame; and mechanism confirmation is drawn only after deployment is immutable, using pre-specified outcome-free challenge cells. Raw similarity scales are not compared across descriptor families, automatic measurement failure remains a design state, and every unequal pair-inclusion probability is retained for the accepted Hájek analysis.

The accepted deployment target population is the realized, deduplicated canonical confirmation frame. The design does not support a primary claim about arbitrary image pairs, other retrieval depths, other descriptor queues, or other species. Camera, site, illumination, identity truth, reviewer outcomes, oracle annotations, fitted predictions, and routes cannot enter primary sampling under the accepted contract.

These acceptances replace the historical 1,200-pair default with a 2,000-analyzable-pair four-stage programme and establish its full-strength review and sampling design. On 16 July 2026, the retained 3,000-image quality audit, the 160-pair historical exclusion register, and the image-strata preflight passed; the official seed was then frozen once and used to create an exact zero-overlap 1,000/1,000/1,000 image-role allocation. The record still requires complete within-role outcome-free pair measurements, a passed post-partition cell-capacity audit, exact inclusion-probability and canonical-pair crossing manifests, and passed role-separation and access-control audits. No formal pair sample, reviewer packet, or outcome collection is authorized until those remaining requirements are complete.
