# Task 03 Operational Evidence Addendum

Status: evidence update complete; it does not authorize a numerical freeze.
Workstream: 04 — Dual-Sample Confirmatory Design.

The final timed operational rehearsal return is archived at `archive/pferi_v2/task_runs/dual_sample_confirmation/2026-07-15_timed_operational_rehearsal_v2/operational_return_audit.json`. It passed the frozen return contract with 72 completed records from three opaque participant codes. Each code completed 24 assigned tasks, all returned tasks matched the restricted allocation, the logs contained no extra response columns, and the audit found neither duplicate participant-packet work nor technical interruptions. The public interface and the restricted allocation therefore demonstrated a workable route for conducting an outcome-free operational rehearsal without collecting a semantic image judgement.

The observed timing evidence is deliberately narrow. The return auditor reported a P95 elapsed duration of 24.270 seconds. Independent descriptive checking found a conventional median of 9.388 seconds, a mean of 12.233 seconds, two durations below one second, and one duration of 131.791 seconds. None of those observations violates the frozen maximum-duration rule, and none may be removed after review. The auditor's displayed even-sample median is currently the upper central order statistic rather than the conventional average of the two central values; because the pass rule does not depend on the median, this does not alter the PASS disposition. A future cost model must state and use one summary convention consistently.

This evidence supports the limited claim that the implemented interface, task allocation, and logging path operated successfully for the observed rehearsal. It does not measure the time needed to make an identity or reviewability judgement, because participants were instructed not to make one. The project owner subsequently attested that the three opaque codes represented three different people. That attestation resolves the narrow question of whether the codes were assigned to distinct people, but it does not establish reviewer independence, domain competence, label reliability, formal-review nonresponse, invalid-label frequency, or adjudication frequency. The observed seconds therefore remain interface evidence and are not used to estimate semantic-review time.

For the binding decision record, the project owner may cite this addendum as evidence of operational feasibility only. The experiment does not assign an expected semantic-review duration or a collection window. Actual elapsed time, disagreement, adjudication, missingness, and technical failure will be reported from the formal immutable logs after execution. This addendum cannot supply the practical Brier increment, the primary superiority rule, the confirmation allocation, the graph-aware planning scenario, the sampling strata, or the official seed.
