# Pre-Outcome Sampling-Strata Design Proposal

Status: accepted by the project owner pre-outcome on 16 July 2026; the one-time official seed and exact 1,000/1,000/1,000 image assignment are now frozen, while pair sampling and outcome packets remain blocked.  
Applies to: Workstream 04 Task 03 and the final execution stage of Workstream 03.  
Prepared: 15 July 2026 before any v2 outcome packet or outcome review.

## Scientific role of the design

The sampling rule must protect two different forms of validity. The deployment-confirmation sample must preserve a known probability relationship to the frozen candidate queue, because it supports the primary Brier comparison, calibration, risk, coverage, and workflow claims. The mechanism-confirmation sample must deliberately expose conditions under which automatic pair evidence could fail, because a representative sample alone may contain too few difficult cases to explain the model. These aims cannot be satisfied by one undifferentiated sample. A mechanism-enriched result cannot estimate queue prevalence, while a representative deployment result cannot establish that every proposed mechanism was adequately challenged.

This separation follows the general prediction-model principle that evaluation data and case mix must be described transparently and that non-representative samples limit generalisability (Collins et al., 2024, doi: [10.1136/bmj-2023-078378](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-078378)). It also matches camera-trap identification guidance that difficult photographs, unclassifiable cases, observer disagreement, and reconciliation procedures must be visible rather than silently removed (Choo et al., 2020, doi: [10.1016/j.gecco.2020.e01294](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gecco.2020.e01294)). The design therefore combines a representative probability sample with a separate mechanism-coverage sample and retains exact inclusion probabilities whenever sampling is unequal.

## Eligible universe and prohibited information

The sampling unit is one eligible canonical unordered pair in the frozen CzechLynx dual-descriptor queue. Multiple descriptor or directional memberships remain hidden design metadata and never create additional review rows. The eligible pair universe must exclude every pair previously used in a measurement pilot, interface dry run, timed operational rehearsal, or retired rehearsal; every pair with an unavailable endpoint; and every pair whose endpoints are assigned to different image-information roles. These exclusions are fixed before outcome access and must be enumerated with reason codes.

Strata may use only outcome-free information available under the measurement contract. Permitted inputs are canonical image and pair identifiers, graph degree, frozen descriptor membership, candidate rank, within-descriptor similarity percentile, and retained automatic image- or pair-evidence measurements generated by the frozen Workstream 02 procedures. Reviewer outcomes, identity truth, future PF-ERI predictions, action routes, oracle annotations, adjudication results, and any confirmation result are prohibited. Raw similarity values from MegaDescriptor and DINOv2 must not be compared directly because their scales are model-specific; comparisons across descriptor families use percentiles computed within descriptor, role frame, and frozen queue snapshot.

## Image allocation before pair sampling

Before images are assigned to information roles, the retained automatic image-quality pipeline must be run on all 3,000 images. Each image receives an outcome-free graph-degree quintile and one of three quality states. The quality state is `measurement_failure` when any retained required image measurement is invalid, `quality_stress` for the bottom quintile of a deterministic quality-bottleneck rank, and `ordinary` otherwise. The quality-bottleneck rank is the minimum of the direction-corrected percentile ranks for native pixel count, sharpness, and inverse exposure-clipping fraction. The clipping measurement retains its Workstream 02 construct caveat: it is an operational channel-extreme fraction, not a validated measure of photographic exposure quality. All percentile ties are resolved by opaque image ID so that recomputation is deterministic.

The cross-product of five graph-degree groups and three quality states forms at most fifteen image-allocation cells. A constrained blocked randomization assigns exactly 1,000 images to development, 1,000 to calibration, and 1,000 to confirmation. Cell-by-role integer quotas are determined before the random draw by a largest-remainder transportation allocation whose row totals equal observed cell sizes and whose column totals are exactly 1,000. Images are then randomly permuted within each cell using the later official seed. Every integer tie is resolved by the ascending cryptographic hash of the contract version, official seed, role, and cell ID. Empty cells remain empty and measurement-failure images remain in the experiment. This step balances graph connectivity and broad automatic image quality without using identity or outcome information.

## Common retrieval strata

After image allocation, the canonical frame for each role contains only pairs whose two endpoints belong to that role. Every retained pair receives a descriptor-support category of `megadescriptor_only`, `dinov2_only`, or `both`. It also receives a best-rank band based on the minimum candidate rank across all of its frozen memberships: ranks 1–5, 6–10, or 11–20. Their cross-product forms nine common retrieval cells. For similarity calculations, the canonical pair score for one descriptor is the maximum similarity across that pair's available directions for the descriptor, after which the score is converted to a percentile among pairs carrying the same descriptor in the same role frame. The full pre-partition queue currently contains at least 1,261 pairs in every retrieval cell, but that global count is not evidence that every post-partition cell is adequate; the official frame audit must recompute all nine counts after image assignment.

The frozen automatic pair-evidence pipeline must then run on every eligible within-role pair before any pair sample is selected. This requirement is essential because current local-match and quality results cover only the measurement pilot. Automatic measurement failure is retained as a design state and never converted into a pair exclusion merely to improve apparent model performance. The complete outcome-free measurement manifest, extractor versions, checksums, failure codes, and frame hashes must be frozen before the pair-sampling seed is applied.

## Development and calibration samples

The 445-pair development collection is designed for learning rather than prevalence estimation. Each development-frame pair is classified by the nine common retrieval cells and an automatic-evidence state. The evidence state is `measurement_failure` when a required retained automatic value is invalid. Among valid rows, `evidence_stress` means that the minimum endpoint native-pixel percentile, minimum endpoint sharpness percentile, inverse maximum endpoint clipping percentile, or local-match-coverage percentile lies in its role-frame bottom quintile, or that a dual-descriptor pair's absolute descriptor-percentile difference lies in its top quintile. All other valid rows are `ordinary`. The resulting maximum of twenty-seven cells receives near-equal allocation by a deterministic water-filling rule. One selection slot is added to every nonempty cell with remaining capacity until 445 slots are assigned; the starting cell and every exact tie are ordered by the cryptographic hash of the contract version, official seed, and cell ID. A seeded simple random sample is then drawn within each cell. This design gives model development broad outcome-free feature coverage; it does not support a prevalence claim.

The 445-pair calibration collection must approximate the target queue rather than maximize difficulty. It therefore uses proportional allocation across the nine common retrieval cells. Integer cell counts are assigned by the Hamilton largest-remainder rule from the observed calibration-frame counts, with every nonempty cell receiving at least one pair when feasible. Exact remainder ties are resolved by the cryptographic hash of the contract version, official seed, analytical role, and cell ID. Sampling is without replacement inside each cell. The hidden manifest records the frame size, selected size, and first-order inclusion probability for every pair. Calibration outcomes may fit only the pre-specified calibration map and action thresholds after the development specification is frozen.

## Deployment and mechanism confirmation samples

Inside the confirmation image role, the 889-pair deployment sample must be selected before the mechanism sample. It uses the same nine common retrieval cells and proportional Hamilton allocation, followed by sampling without replacement within cells. The primary finite-population target is the frozen, deduplicated canonical confirmation frame produced by the accepted image partition, not directed retrieval exposures and not all logically possible image pairs. Exact first-order inclusion probabilities conditional on that frozen frame are retained. If integer allocation makes probabilities unequal, the primary deployment mean uses the already accepted Hájek-normalized inverse-probability weighting; inverse-probability estimators are specifically designed to retain a population-mean interpretation when sampling probabilities are known (Datta and Polson, 2022, [arXiv:2204.14121](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.14121)). Extension from the realized confirmation frame to the full 3,000-image candidate graph depends on the pre-outcome randomized image allocation and must be described as generalisation rather than as the primary finite-population estimand.

Only after the deployment sample is immutable may the 445-pair mechanism sample be drawn from the remaining confirmation pairs. Each remaining pair is assigned, in fixed precedence order, to one of six mutually exclusive challenge classes: automatic-measurement failure; image-quality stress; bottom-quintile local correspondence; descriptor exclusivity; top-quintile dual-descriptor percentile disagreement; or ordinary reference. Each challenge class is crossed with the three best-rank bands, producing at most eighteen mechanism cells. Near-equal allocation uses the same deterministic water-filling rule as development, followed by random sampling without replacement inside cells. The hierarchy is an allocation device, not a causal model: later mechanism analyses must use the underlying continuous measurements and must not claim that membership in one challenge class isolates a single cause.

## Sparse cells, dependence, and missingness

No stratum may be redefined after outcomes. Proportional samples use the fixed largest-remainder rule. Equal-coverage samples use seeded, hash-ordered water filling and skip only cells with zero remaining capacity. If the requested total cannot be filled, the process stops with `INSUFFICIENT_FRAME_CAPACITY`; investigators may not merge a convenient cell, relax a measurement threshold, or substitute a previously viewed pair. Any revised rule requires a new version, new outcome-free audit, and explicit owner acceptance before a seed is chosen.

Pair observations are allowed to share image endpoints because an 889-pair sample from 1,000 confirmation images cannot generally be image-disjoint. The sample audit must report the number of unique image nodes, sampled pair-degree distribution, maximum sampled degree, and descriptor-cell counts. Shared-image dependence is handled by the accepted dyadic cluster-robust interval rather than by pretending that edges are independent or by choosing a favourable sample after inspecting graph statistics. Nonresponse, invalid review, and technical failure retain the originally selected pair and status code; replacement pairs are prohibited unless a separate pre-specified reserve order was generated by the same cell-level random permutation before outcomes.

## Unavailable covariates and claim boundary

Camera, site, illumination, and verified identity are not present in the current neutral freeze manifest and therefore cannot appear in the primary strata. Their absence limits subgroup and transport claims but does not justify inferring them from filenames or image appearance. If an authorized field becomes available before the official seed, adding it requires a versioned amendment, a new capacity audit, and renewed owner acceptance. Restricted identity information may support the already defined identity-disjoint sensitivity analysis, but it cannot alter primary pair selection after outcomes or enter the reviewer interface.

This design estimates performance only for the frozen CzechLynx top-20 dual-descriptor canonical queue. It does not establish performance for arbitrary image pairs, other retrieval depths, other descriptor families, other sites, or other felid species. A future species adapter must repeat local sampling and validation rather than inherit these strata as proof of transfer.

## Acceptance and next gate

The project owner accepted this proposal on 16 July 2026 before any v2 outcome packet, official seed, image assignment, or pair sample existed. This acceptance freezes the variables, bin definitions, allocation algorithms, sampling order, sparse-cell rule, target-population boundary, and prohibited inputs. The primary deployment target is the realized, deduplicated canonical confirmation frame; it is not arbitrary image pairs, other retrieval depths, or another species.

The pre-seed gates passed on 16 July 2026. The project then froze official seed `af9a4f6fa244186aaed25511ef7c481ca22bb8371c13cbf715853dc6f47f0032` with reroll prohibited and generated an exact 1,000-development/1,000-calibration/1,000-confirmation image allocation with zero cross-role image overlap. Only after this partition exists can the project compute frozen automatic pair measurements for every eligible within-role pair and run the post-partition capacity, canonical-pair crossing, and probability-accounting gates. Pair selection uses namespaced derivatives of the same frozen seed only after those gates pass. This ordering is necessary because a within-role pair frame does not exist before image allocation; the frozen image split does not itself authorize pair sampling or an outcome packet.
