# Task 15I Independent Redevelopment Design Freeze

Status: `FROZEN_OUTCOME_FREE_INDEPENDENT_REDEVELOPMENT_DESIGN`

Task 15I establishes a prospective route after Task 15H found no qualified development model. It does not rerun Task 15E, reopen the existing development labels for selection, or reassign the existing calibration, deployment-confirmation, or mechanism-confirmation stages. The design builder read only outcome-free covariates, graph and sampling metadata, the Task 15H nonqualification record, and synthetic outcomes generated internally for design calculations. The design audit reports `real_outcomes_used=false` and `locked_stage_outcomes_accessed=false`.

The prior development sample contained 445 pairs in 116 endpoint-image components, with one component containing 68 pairs. This structure leaves a realistic possibility that one component affects an outer-fold conclusion disproportionately. The new design therefore defines the endpoint-image connected component as the unit of independent replication. It requires at least 1,200 analyzable pairs in at least 300 components, with a nominal four pairs per component, no more than eight pairs per component, and no image endpoint appearing in more than six selected pairs. Five outer folds must each contain at least 60 components. New images and new canonical pairs must have zero overlap with every existing analytical role.

The fixed-lambda stress simulation used the frozen outcome-free development covariates, the registered ten synthetic mechanisms, square-root inverse-probability weighting, and twelve synthetic replicates per mechanism. It compared the predeclared grid from 0.1 through 100 and selected lambda 100 by minimax median full-model Brier regret, with stronger shrinkage as the exact-tie rule. This selection does not use any observed label. Lambda 100 is fixed prospectively for both P3 and P5; neither outer-fold nor inner-fold retuning is permitted in the future independent redevelopment execution.

The component-level operating-characteristic simulation assumed a paired-loss-difference standard deviation of 0.12 and intracomponent correlation of 0.10. At the recommended 300-component, 1,200-pair design, the design effect is 1.30 and the effective pair count is approximately 923. For a synthetic true Brier increment of 0.01, the probability that the development point estimate reaches the predeclared 0.005 increment is 0.8976. The probability that a two-sided 95% lower bound exceeds 0.005 is only 0.2438. This distinction is deliberate: Task 15I is a development qualification design, whereas the later confirmation rule retains its stricter graph-aware lower-bound requirement. The simulation is conditional on its synthetic assumptions and is not a prediction that P5 will achieve an increment of 0.01.

The future independent redevelopment result must pass all frozen gates: fixed lambda 100 for P3 and P5, finite unit-interval probabilities, zero endpoint leakage, a design-weighted P3-minus-P5 Brier increment of at least 0.005, nonnegative increment in at least four of five outer folds, absolute weighted calibration intercept no greater than 0.20, and weighted calibration slope from 0.80 through 1.20. P3 may not become an automatic fallback if P5 fails, and sensitivity or exploratory routes remain ineligible for promotion.

Task 15I-A/B does not authorize outcome collection, calibration, or confirmation. The next required work is Task 15I-C: build a genuinely image-disjoint and pair-disjoint redevelopment sampling manifest, verify component and stratum capacity against this frozen design, then freeze new folds, preprocessing, and an execution package before opening any new development outcome. If the future independent development result fails any qualification gate, the P5 confirmation route terminates without accessing a locked-stage outcome.
