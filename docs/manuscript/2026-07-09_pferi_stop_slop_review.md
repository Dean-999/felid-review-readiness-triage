## Phrases Intentionally Preserved

The manuscript retains some negative boundary language because the project's scientific credibility depends on it. The following claim boundaries should not be removed for style alone:

`PF-ERI does not train a new descriptor or assign identity.`

`The endpoint was human reviewability and evidential admissibility, not identity accuracy or retrieval mAP.`

`Bobcat analyses are restricted to unlabeled transfer-stress and workflow allocation.`

`The manuscript cannot report Bobcat identity accuracy, false-match accuracy, mAP, MRR, or top-k identity retrieval without audited Bobcat labels.`

These sentences are not rhetorical "not X, but Y" flourishes. They are claim gates.
