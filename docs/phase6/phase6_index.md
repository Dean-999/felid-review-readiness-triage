# Phase 6 Index

Phase 6 is the largest documentation cluster. It should be read as the project's mathematical, validation, annotation, and claim-boundary expansion phase, not as a single linear report.

## What Phase 6 Did

Phase 6 reframed PF-ERI from a simple review-readiness score into a controlled evidence utility framework. It explored:

- mathematical model options;
- factor-to-risk modeling;
- identity-holdout validation;
- calibrated risk and clustered uncertainty;
- simulated open-set policy behavior;
- annotation expansion and unique-500 review workflows;
- species scope and claim boundaries;
- whether training, calibration, or review control should become the next endpoint.
