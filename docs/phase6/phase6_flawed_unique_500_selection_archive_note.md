# Phase 6 Flawed Unique-500 Selection Archive Note

outputs/czechlynx/phase6/archive/flawed_unique_500_selection_20260613/

## Reason For Archive

The previous Phase 6 unique-500 image-level annotation workflow was archived because the selected set over-enriched extreme hard cases. The first batch contained too many almost-black or extremely low-information images.

That composition is not appropriate for PF-ERI factor-to-risk analysis. A useful 500-image expansion needs clear, medium, low-but-annotatable, and extreme-hard images in controlled proportions. Over-selecting near-empty images would bias the new labels toward exclusion/defer cases and weaken estimation of normal and gradual degradation behavior.

## What Was Archived

The archived material includes the old active unique-500 review images, old annotation templates and working files, old assisted annotation workspace, old batch package outputs, and previous candidate-selection outputs tied to the flawed 500-image selection.

## What Was Not Changed

Raw CzechLynx data were not deleted or modified.

Earlier frozen Phase 4 labels remain untouched.

Phase 5 and other Phase 6 scientific outputs outside the flawed active annotation workflow remain available.
