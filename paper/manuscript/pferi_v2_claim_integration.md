# PF-ERI v2 Claim Integration Through Task17

This document is the controlled manuscript-facing integration of PF-ERI v2 evidence through Task17. It does not replace the historical v1 manuscript, and it is not yet a complete submission-ready negative-result paper. Figure 1 in the paper working folder serves as the graphical abstract for the scientific object by showing the transition from descriptor retrieval to pair-level evidence admission and expert review. Figures 2 through 4 remain historical v1 display items and cannot be presented as v2 confirmation figures.

## Current Evidence Status

PF-ERI v2 studies a post-retrieval pair-level evidence-admission decision. The system estimates whether a descriptor-retrieved image pair contains sufficiently comparable visual evidence for responsible human review. It does not replace the upstream descriptor, assign animal identity, or convert reviewability into identity truth. This definition remains the central manuscript claim and preserves the distinction between visual similarity and evidential admissibility.

The independent Task15I development route provided 1,600 reviewed pairs in 400 endpoint-disjoint components. Under the prospectively frozen component-disjoint evaluation, the P5 full model reduced weighted Brier score relative to the P3 active control by 0.0058629 and showed a nonnegative increment in all five outer folds. Task15J therefore froze P5 and P3 with the outcome-free fixed regularization value of 100. This result supports development-stage qualification of the P5 feature set within the independent redevelopment contract. It does not establish independent confirmation performance, identity accuracy, or deployment utility.

Task15L completed the prespecified apparent probability calibration for the frozen P3 and P5 models on 448 calibration pairs. The calibration procedure fitted only the permitted intercept and slope transformations, produced valid calibrated probabilities, and did not reselect features, alter regularization, or refit the original models. These results establish that the frozen models have a reproducible calibrated scoring form for subsequent execution. They do not independently validate predictive performance outside the calibration sample.

The authoritative Task15M external execution received the bounded status `PASS_TASK15M_EXECUTION_VALIDATION`. The returned package covered 889 candidate pairs, of which 252 had the complete supported measurement path and 637 were recorded as unsupported under the frozen execution contract. The execution verified 815 images, 252 local-match rows, and 504 calibrated P3/P5 prediction rows without a recorded execution failure in the supported path. Descriptor dimensions and artifact hashes matched their audits. This result establishes successful execution, coverage, and artifact integrity.

Task15M did not access confirmation outcomes, and Task15N did not calculate Brier score or any other outcome-performance metric. Task17 subsequently opened the accepted deployment-confirmation outcomes exactly once through the frozen restricted linkage. The analysis used only the 252 supported pairs, the frozen Task15J models, and the fixed Task15L calibration parameters. P3 had a Hajek-weighted Brier score of 0.307246 and P5 had a score of 0.509116, producing a P3-minus-P5 increment of -0.201870. The two-sided 95% dyadic cluster-robust interval using original physical image endpoints was -0.224671 to -0.179068. The predeclared success rule required the lower endpoint to exceed 0.005. It failed decisively. P5 therefore did not establish independent confirmation superiority over P3 in the supported Task15M subset.

The status `PASS_TASK15M_EXECUTION_VALIDATION` remains an execution result and must not be conflated with external predictive validation. Task17 supplies the separate primary outcome result, which is `FAIL_PRIMARY_CONFIRMATION` and supports the Workstream 05 decision `v2_not_confirmed`. The negative confirmation result does not establish identity accuracy, deployment utility, workflow efficiency, or automatic identity assignment. It also does not erase the bounded Task15I development-screen result; rather, it shows that development qualification did not generalize to the frozen independent confirmation analysis.

## Relationship To The Historical Manuscript

The historical v1 manuscript remains exploratory and under submission lock. Its 400-pair model comparisons, reviewer-agreement summaries, routing simulations, and associated figures preserve provenance but are not PF-ERI v2 confirmation evidence. Those numerical results may be discussed only as historical hypothesis-generating work. They cannot be pooled with Task15I development data, Task15L calibration data, or Task15M execution artifacts, and they cannot fill the missing independent confirmation-performance result.

## Manuscript Disposition

The manuscript may report the PF-ERI scientific object, the bounded independent development qualification, completion of the frozen calibration procedure, successful Task15M execution validation, and the Task17 negative primary confirmation result. It must report that P5 failed the frozen independent confirmation criterion and must not translate the Task15I development gain into an external-performance, identity-accuracy, or deployment-utility claim. The appropriate v2 scientific disposition is a transparent negative or mixed-result account, not a confirmed-performance paper.
