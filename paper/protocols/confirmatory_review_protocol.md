# PF-ERI v2 Confirmatory Review Protocol

Status: v2 design draft; no v2 outcome review has begun.  
Date: 2026-07-10

## Objective

This protocol specifies the forward-looking CzechLynx confirmation study for PF-ERI v2. The study evaluates whether a descriptor-retrieved image pair contains sufficient comparable visual evidence for responsible human individual-level review. The reviewer endpoint is evidential admissibility or reviewability, not identity assignment. The protocol is governed by `PROJECT_RULES.md`; no historical Phase18N review form, sample, or output may be used as a v2 confirmation artifact.

## Canonical Pair Universe and Sampling

The sampling universe is constructed from frozen CzechLynx candidate queues generated by the two primary strong descriptors. Each physical image pair is represented by one canonical unordered-pair identifier, while all query-direction and descriptor memberships are retained as metadata. The manifest records source-image identifiers, descriptor name, rank, similarity, known same/different identity status, image availability, camera/site and day/night metadata where available, feature availability, and the exclusion reason for every omitted candidate. A pair cannot enter more than one v2 sample, even if both descriptors retrieve it.

The formal target is 1,200 unique unordered pairs. The mechanism-confirmation sample contains 400 pairs chosen to ensure pre-specified coverage across descriptor family, similarity or rank, independent image-quality state, laterality or viewpoint, visible-evidence state, same/different identity status, camera/site, and illumination state whenever those fields exist. This sample tests the measurement mechanism; it is not a prevalence estimate. The deployment-queue sample contains 800 additional pairs drawn with retained inclusion probabilities from the frozen candidate queue, with 400 memberships from each primary descriptor after cross-descriptor deduplication. It estimates calibration, coverage, routing, time, and cost in a realistic queue.

Before any v2 outcome review, an auditable power-and-cost simulation will freeze the exact target, seed, stratum quotas, and any necessary nonresponse reserve. A change from 1,200 must be made before outcome labels are viewed and must record its statistical and operational rationale. The selection script must produce a manifest, a duplicate audit, image-availability audit, identity and image-component summary, and a blind-export audit.

## Feature Measurement

Feature measurement is conducted by a team or workflow that does not create outcome-reviewability labels. The planned feature schema includes automatically measurable animal coverage, image resolution, sharpness and exposure estimates, infrared or illumination state, visible body-region and laterality estimates, occlusion, visible patterned-area estimates, local-match coverage, descriptor disagreement, feature uncertainty, and inference failures. Each field has a declared missing-value representation. Human structural annotation may be used to validate the feature measurement but is an oracle measurement, not an automatically deployable input.

Before confirmation collection, a separate difficult-case measurement pilot will test variation, missingness, inter-rater reliability, agreement between automatic and human measurements, and computation time. A feature that is constant, unavailable at inference time, or unreliable under the locked pilot definition is excluded from the primary automatic model. Neither the feature team nor any automatic feature export may receive outcome labels before the feature schema is frozen.

## Blinded Outcome Review

Every sampled pair receives two independent first-pass reviews. The reviewer interface displays only the two rendered images, a neutral pair identifier, the reviewability decision, a reason field, confidence, optional notes, and a timestamp. It must not display or make inferable the descriptor name, similarity, rank, PF-ERI score, feature values, route, sampling stratum, identity truth, existing labels, comments, filenames that encode metadata, or a sortable hidden field containing those values.

Reviewers classify a pair as `review_ready`, `not_review_ready`, or `uncertain`. A third blinded adjudicator reviews only cases that meet the pre-specified disagreement rule. The adjudicator does not see the first-pass decisions. All raw form submissions, reviewer assignments, interface source version and hash, image-rendering transformation, timestamps, and failed or restarted sessions are retained as immutable artifacts. A derived majority label and adjudicated label are analysis products, not replacements for the raw records.

## Known Identity, Confidentiality, and Leakage Audit

Known same/different identity status is stored separately from the reviewer-facing packet. It may be used to create the locked sampling strata and after label lock to report pre-specified subgroup analyses. It must not enter the review form or reviewer instructions. Before collection, a person who is not reviewing labels performs a leakage audit by inspecting the rendered interface, exported CSV columns, filenames, browser state, and possible sort or filter operations. A detected leak invalidates the affected batch until the source of leakage, potentially exposed records, remediation, and disposition are recorded.

## Review Completion and Data Lock

Reviewers work independently and do not discuss individual cases during the first pass. Review completion is followed by an audit of raw-log completeness, duplicate pair exposure, time anomalies, reviewer assignment, blinding, image availability, and response validity. Only after those audits pass may labels be aggregated according to the pre-specified analysis plan. The feature schema, model code, split manifest, and threshold policy must already be versioned by then. The v2 confirmation partition remains concealed from model-selection decisions until the single planned analysis run.

## Claim Boundary

This protocol can support a narrowly scoped CzechLynx evidence-admission claim only if its pre-specified measurement, blinding, modelling, calibration, and workflow conditions are met. It cannot support a claim that PF-ERI assigns animal identity, improves retrieval ranking, validates Bobcat, provides a universal cross-domain guarantee, or saves time unless the corresponding prospective evidence has been collected and analysed under its own locked contract.
